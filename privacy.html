YOUR DATA, YOUR CALL

Privacy, in plain words.

Chatshit is a small chat website. This page explains what stays on your device and what is shared when you connect services.

Effective 6 October 2026

Spotify

When you choose Connect, Chatshit asks Spotify for permission to read your currently playing track. The track title, artist, Spotify link and cover image are fetched for your own browser and shown only in your Notes view. Chatshit does not send this live playback data to its shared database.

Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored in this browser so the connection can continue on this device. Choose Disconnect in the Notes editor to delete those local tokens and stop playback requests. You can also remove Chatshit from your connected apps in your Spotify account.

If you choose Add current song link and then share your Note, you are choosing to share that Spotify URL with the Chatshit room. Shared Notes and their links are visible to signed-in room members and expire after 24 hours.

Messages and Notes

Chatshit has one public community room; it does not provide private one-to-one chats. Without a configured Supabase project, posting is disabled. When the backend is configured, messages and Notes are stored in Supabase and can be read by signed-in room members. Avoid posting private or sensitive information there.

The People directory lists display names and account creation dates for signed-in community profiles. Email addresses are not requested or displayed. Each user can update only their own profile.

Images and Stories

Images are stored in a private Supabase bucket. Signed-in members can view them through temporary links that expire after seven days; anyone you forward a link to can open it before it expires. Stories are visible to signed-in members for 24 hours, then disappear from the app. Expired Story records and their stored image files are cleaned up when a member next opens the app.

Only JPEG, PNG, and WebP files up to 5 MB are accepted. Do not share images you consider private.

Third-party services

Spotify handles Spotify sign-in and playback requests. Supabase hosts shared chat data when enabled. The website may also load its fonts and the Supabase browser library from their providers.

Questions or data removal

Disconnect Spotify in the Notes editor to clear the Spotify tokens stored on this device. To ask about this website or shared data, contact the project maintainer through the project issue tracker.
